**Dissemination**

The Dissemination page is the email communication hub of iRAINS — used by HQ, MC, and RMC users to send rainfall data and reports to recipients via email. It is organised into three sub-sections accessible from a sidebar: **Send Email**, **Email Group**, and **Email Log**.

Access: HQ, MC, and SP users.

---

## Navigation — Sidebar Menu

When you land on the Dissemination page, a sidebar on the left shows three navigation links. Click any link to open that sub-section in the main content area.

## Sub-Section 1: Send Email

∙ **Purpose:** Compose and send an email with rainfall data or map attachments to one or more recipients — either individuals or pre-defined email groups.

∙ **How to access:** Click "Send Email" in the sidebar.

∙ **What you do here:**
    → Step 1: Select recipients (individual addresses or an existing email group).
    → Step 2: Choose the date or data to attach (rainfall map, statistics file, etc.).
    → Step 3: Write or review the email subject and body.
    → Step 4: Click Send to dispatch the email. A confirmation message appears on success.

## Sub-Section 2: Email Group

∙ **Purpose:** Create and manage named groups of email recipients. Once a group is defined, it can be selected in the Send Email page to address multiple people at once — saving time during routine daily dissemination.

∙ **How to access:** Click "Email Group" in the sidebar.

∙ **What you do here:**
    → Step 1: Click "Add Group" or similar action to create a new group.
    → Step 2: Enter a group name and add email addresses to the group.
    → Step 3: Save the group.
    → Step 4: To edit or delete an existing group, find it in the group list and use the Edit or Delete buttons.

## Sub-Section 3: Email Log

∙ **Purpose:** A read-only audit trail of all emails sent through iRAINS — who sent what, to whom, and when. Useful for tracking dissemination history and confirming that automated or manual emails were successfully dispatched.

∙ **How to access:** Click "Email Log" in the sidebar.

∙ **What you see here:**
    ∙ A table of all sent emails with columns for: Date/Time sent, Sender, Recipients, Subject, Attachment (if any), Status (Sent / Failed).
    ∙ You can scroll or paginate through the log.
    ∙ No actions are available — this is a view-only log.
